# Tokyo SHIFT agent guidance

## Character and narrative continuity

Before creating or changing character dialogue, cutscenes, interactions, magazine narrative or story content, read:
- [Character Bible v1](docs/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md)
- [Narrative authoring guidance](docs/NARRATIVE_AUTHORING.md)

Use those repository documents as the continuity source. Preserve individual voices and knowledge boundaries across Career and future Story Mode.

The bible's adopted foundation is usable now. Explicitly unresolved, conditional and provisional items retain their labels; do not silently decide the protagonist-identity model or reassign the shared opening history.

Deeper backstories, concealed affiliations and antagonists can be added through later story design. Do not invent them incidentally during unrelated work or disclose private facts in Career dialogue without a valid knowledge/reveal condition.

When authorised narrative work creates a durable fact, update the canon and affected knowledge/relationship entries. Keep variable save outcomes distinct from fixed past. Documentation does not itself implement runtime narrative gates.

This guidance adds no deployment approval requirement and does not authorise unrelated runtime changes.
