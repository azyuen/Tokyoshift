# Ginza Motor Gallery & GMG Racing — current character and business canon

**Approved: 30 October 2026, R742.** Authoritative for Aruken and Sayaka's commercial position and GMG Racing in the later Career timeline. Supersedes older descriptions of Aruken as a sole billionaire gallery owner or personal owner of every collector vehicle. It does not rewrite the Aruken ten-chapter prequel, the earlier Career revelation gates, gameplay, or established results.

## Relationship and identities
- **Aruken Denzaki** (internal ID `arkonDen`) and **Sayaka Fujieda** are established, committed, long-term romantic partners by Career Mode and 50/50 business partners. They express affection through familiar banter, mutual respect, support and small gestures; marriage, exact ages, past partners and public relationship labels stay undefined.
- Aruken leads mechanical engineering, specialist tuning, technical restoration and safety verification. Sayaka leads curation, client relations, sales, consignment and race strategy. Neither is merely the other's employee, benefactor or romantic reward.
- Sayaka genuinely enjoyed teaching at Oshiage after leaving the pressures of professional motorsport. Aruken encourages her return to the competitive strategy work she misses, through a venture **both choose**. Their career decisions benefit each other.
- Their business is respected and comfortably successful, not an unlimited-finance billionaire empire. The Arkon Den developer login, debug privileges, 900-million-yen owner shortcut and test drives are **out-of-world tooling**; they do not establish Aruken's personal wealth or portfolio.

## Business, vehicles and ownership
- **Ginza Motor Gallery (GMG):** Aruken and Sayaka equally own its operating business. Sayaka is commercial/curatorial lead; Aruken is engineering/technical lead.
- **Shinonome Warehouse HQ:** a jointly operated specialist workshop business, with Aruken directing engineering. No assertion that they own the building or underlying real estate; Daichi receives a genuine favourable lease, quietly arranged through office paperwork. He has not met Aruken before the first Pro Season sponsor reveal.
- **Vehicle provenance:** GMG works with consignments, brokerage, customer builds, owner-authorised restorations, occasional demonstration stock and selected vehicles actually purchased by the business. Most extraordinarily valuable gallery cars need not belong personally to either founder. A vault drive is permitted supervised access, never a transfer of ownership. Sayaka's black RWB Porsche remains her personal car, gifted in Prelude Chapter 6.
- **Liberty Walk collaboration:** LBWK supplies styling/bodykit expertise, while GMG/Aruken provides engineering, technical integration and verification, and Sayaka handles customer relationships, presentation and consignment/sales if required. No changes to build pricing or upgrade mechanics.
- **The two 240Zs:** Aruken sold **his original personally owned and tuned Liberty Walk 240Z** during Prelude Chapter 10 to discharge his obligations. The later Career-only `arkonGinza240z` (RB26-swapped, black-and-gold #16, listed at ¥125 million) is a **different, later GMG × LBWK one-off** using his signature styling. It is a commercial collector/demonstration vehicle; exact client/company ownership is deliberately unspecified. The sanctioned no-stakes Pro sponsor exhibition uses this later car and never reverses the prequel sale.

## GMG Racing: joint and commercially credible
- **GMG Racing is a shared commercial decision, not Aruken giving Sayaka a team.** A modest, budgeted racing sponsorship can generate publicity, technical learning and future clients. It can also lose money; it is not guaranteed profitable.
- **Personal meaning:** Sayaka independently wants to direct strategy again; Aruken is pleased to see her return to a paddock. She helps decide and run the programme, while he supplies genuine specialist and commercial support.
- The existing one-time first Pro Season signing pays **initial participation expenses only**: fees, bond, insurance and facilities. No unlimited player cash, upgrades, unearned wins, subsidies for every event or altered prize economy.
- Sayaka leads competitive strategy; Daichi remains the team's hands-on mechanic; the player and recruited drivers earn their own results. Aruken does not replace Daichi, dictate strategy, arrange race brackets or guarantee titles.
- Pre-reveal: keep Aruken's identity and lease involvement secret. His first in-person meeting with Daichi is at the first Pro Season signing. The Tokyo Champion cameo of Aruken and Sayaka remains a short private aside after the player's earned success; no overt relationship declaration.
