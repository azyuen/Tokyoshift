# Tuning potential: measured development capability

These grades describe the available standard build system, not unlimited real-world engine potential. Factory class and recruitment eligibility do not use this score. No physics or exclusive car settings change.

Score: 45% maximum pace + 20% intermediate pace + 15% versatility + 10% improvement + 10% acquisition/build value. Power, torque, mass, traction, gearing and response affect actual elapsed times rather than being counted twice as separate bonuses.

Pace is the mean cubed ratio of benchmark time to measured time, times 100. Maximum benchmarks: 12 s standing 400 m, 18.5 s standing 800 m, 9.8 s rolling 60 km/h 400 m. Intermediate: 13.5 / 20.8 / 10.8 s. Versatility uses maximum-build 400 m results: low grip 12 s, rating-3 driver 14 s, rolling 100 km/h 8.8 s. These are gameplay reference points, not road-test claims.

Improvement compares stock-to-maximum standing and rolling 400 m ET reduction against 25%, capped at 140 score points. Value uses the first tested stage meeting all of 12.5 s / 19.5 s / 10 s in standing 400 / standing 800 / rolling 400: 100 × (¥4m / donor-plus-hardware-cost)^0.25, bounded 60–120. If no sampled stage reaches the benchmark, maximum-build cost is used and value is reduced continuously by the cubed slowest benchmark/time ratio. This avoids a sudden penalty for narrowly missing a time. This is the cheapest sampled standard stage, not an exhaustive optimized build. Specialist fees, NOS and rental/dyno costs are excluded.

Exceptional ≥115; High ≥100; Moderate ≥85; Low <85. Thresholds express strength against fixed performance references, not equal roster sizes. A high-power heavy car need not receive a higher grade than a responsive lightweight. Cost has limited weight so expensive exceptional platforms can retain their distinction.

Limitations: one imperfect-driver seed, fixed tyre-load model, provisional hardware envelopes and incomplete historical specification provenance. Grades are reproducible for this engineering revision and must be regenerated if physics/economy change. They are not final release approval.
