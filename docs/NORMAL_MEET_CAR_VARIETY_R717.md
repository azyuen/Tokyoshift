# R717 — Normal Meet car variety only

**Scope:** Ordinary three-minute Meet race cards may now show a randomly
chosen car independently of the rival character. Their factory/real street
paint is rolled per offer, including main rivals. A region's preferred factory
classes and existing location favourite models influence selection, but all
regular non-collector stock models have some chance of appearing.

**Performance:** An eight-car weighted shortlist is sampled cheaply before
running the existing approved physical vehicle and tuning-based opponent
matchmaker. This avoids simulating the entire stock roster at each refresh.
Driver skill is still chosen from the authored location ratings; player
difficulty, allowed workshop build ceilings, race type, and physical matching
rules are unchanged. No speed multipliers or new AI settings.

**Career boundaries:** Named characters keep their established signature cars
in seven-race regional challenges, stock crew recruitment, and three-on-three
crew battles, including the existing regulated stock rounds. Street Showdowns
remain exactly R716, including regional class-weighted coupons, Office live
prize lookup, and 15-minute prize rotation.

**Save and cache:** `MEET_ROSTER_VERSION` bumps to R717 solely to retire old
signature-locked transient normal Meet cards. Ongoing career/race data and
separately saved competition state remain untouched. Existing three-minute
Meet refresh and result persistence remain unchanged. Asset cache unchanged.
